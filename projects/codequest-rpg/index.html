BEGINNER'S GUIDE · CHAPTER ONE

One line. One direction. Your decision.

You are the programmer. The goal is visible in the world, but the route is yours to discover.

01 · Choose a direction

The hero understands four variable names: up, right, left, and down.

up ↑left ←◆→ right↓ down
02 · Give it a value

Use = and a positive whole number. This example teaches the syntax, not a level solution:

up = 1

That single line sends the hero one tile upward.

03 · Write your own route

Put different assignments on new lines. The character performs them from top to bottom. You may assign the same direction more than once.

Observe the coordinates, obstacles, crystals, and destination. Correct your code and try again.

Conditions and sensors

Use four spaces inside a branch. clear(), locked() and danger() each take a direction. The log shows which branch ran.

if clear(up):
    up = 1
else:
    left = 1
Movement first · no functions, cycles, enemies or combat.
